About me

I am Gregorius Yura Lorenzo Saputra, an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Engineering at Telkom University. With a strong foundation in digital systems, logic design, and programming, I am passionate about making technical concepts accessible and human centered whether through clear documentation, collaborative projects, or creative educational tools.

My academic journey is complemented by a unique blend of creativity and empathy. I specialize in translating complex binary and hexadecimal logic into intuitive, step-by-step explanations, often supported by visual aids and practical tables. I also enjoy exploring the intersection of technology and storytelling, crafting symbolic rituals and digital art that reflect identity, emotion, and connection.

As a collaborative thinker, I thrive in environments that value iteration, feedback, and interdisciplinary exploration. I am currently focused on developing tools and content that empower students to learn technical material in a more relatable and comforting way.
